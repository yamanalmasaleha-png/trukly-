# Operational Diagnostics, Error Redaction & Release Skew

Centralized diagnostics are implemented in [diagnostics.js](../../lib/diagnostics.js) (server), [diagnostics-client.js](../../public/diagnostics-client.js) (browser), `server/admin-routes.js` (`/api/diagnostics/report` & `/api/admin/diagnostics*`), and `migrations/024_diagnostics_and_reliability.sql` + `025_support_feedback_and_diagnostic_links.sql`.

---

## 1. Security, Allowlist & Redaction Invariants

1. **Strict Allowlists (`lib/diagnostics.js:21-77`)**:
   - `ALLOWED_COMPONENTS`: `browser`, `api`, `worker`, `external_provider`.
   - `ALLOWED_SEVERITIES`: `info`, `warning`, `error`, `critical`.
   - `ALLOWED_OUTCOMES`: `failed`, `outcome_uncertain`, `partial_success`, `temporarily_unavailable`, `degraded`.
   - `ALLOWED_FEATURE_KEYS`: 20 keys at `a4e9f53` (+5 exploration keys on `83181ba`, +4 profile/sharing keys on `feature/private-profiles-greatest-hits`: `customer_profile`, `greatest_hits_collections`, `unlisted_sharing`, `shared_moderation`).
   - `TOP_ONLY_FEATURE_KEYS` (`mod_visible = false` — hidden from `Admin: Mod`): `auth_login`, `invitations`, `email_delivery`, `admin_portal`.
   - `SAFE_CONTEXT_KEYS`: Scalar operational keys only (`http_status`, `phase`, `provider`, `delivery_mode`, `storage_kind`, `viewport_bucket`, `tile_host_id`, `attempt`, `queue_depth`, `is_offline`, `feature_state`, `synthetic_label`, `actor_id`, `vendor_id`, `item_count`, `mime_type`, `collection_id`, `share_id`).
2. **Multi-Pass PII & Secret Scrubbing (`sanitizeDiagnosticText` + `sanitizeLoggedRequestPath`)**:
   - Strips control chars/newlines, emails, Bearer/API tokens (`re_`, `sk_`, `pk.`), URLs with query strings/tokens, `/s/<token>` and `/s/<token>/photo/<attachment>` capability URLs (`-> /s/:token` / `/s/:token/photo/:attachment`), cookie headers, 6-digit OTP patterns, UUIDs in paths, and GPS coordinates. Never stores request/response bodies, DOBs, private notes, recommendation text, or raw share tokens. Expected `404` invalid/expired/revoked `/s/<token>` hits and `403` eligibility denials do not create error incidents.
3. **Untrusted Browser Reports (`POST /api/diagnostics/report`)**:
   - Capped at `4 KiB`, rate-limited per IP (`25` per `600s` default), skips session resolution/cookie refresh, and stamps `trust_level = 'browser_untrusted'`.

## 2. Deduplication, Correlation IDs & Release Awareness

- **Fingerprinting**: `SHA-256(component | feature_key | route_template | error_code)` groups repeated failures into `diagnostic_incident_groups` (`occurrence_count`, `first_seen_at`, `last_seen_at`, `triage_status` in `new|investigating|monitoring|resolved`).
- **Correlation Identifiers (`corr_<16-hex>`)**: Generated by `generateCorrelationId()`; attached to unexpected `>= 500` responses via `res.__bsDiagHook` (`server/app.js:516`) as both `X-Correlation-Id` header and `payload.correlation_id`. Intentional maintenance/pause `503`s (`FEATURE_PAUSED`, `WRITE_PAUSED`, etc.) are excluded so planned pauses do not page operators as crashes.
- **Release Skew Detection**: `recordServerReleaseStartup()` records active `BITESHARK_RELEASE_SHA` + `CLIENT_BUNDLE_VERSION` (`bs-ui-2026.11.20`) in `diagnostic_releases`. `public/diagnostics-client.js` inspects `X-BiteShark-Release` and `X-BiteShark-Client-Bundle` on API responses to label incidents `current_release` vs `older_cached_tab`.

---

## 3. Bounded Retention & Support-Ticket Linking

- **Bounded VPS Storage (`getDiagnosticsRetentionPolicy`)**: Defaults to `15` samples/group (`30s` sample cooldown), `500` max groups, `14d` sample retention, `30d` resolved-group retention, `60d` open-group retention (append-only `diagnostic_incident_notes` preserved independently). Pruned periodically by `scripts/run-pager-worker.cjs` and manually via `POST /api/admin/diagnostics/cleanup` (`Admin: Top`).
- **Support-Ticket Linking (`support_issue_diagnostic_links`)**:
  - Auto-suggests unconfirmed links when a customer submits a support ticket carrying a matching `correlation_id`.
  - Staff confirm/unlink via `POST /api/admin/issues/diagnostics` and can preview + broadcast sanitized public status updates to up to 25 open linked tickets via `POST /api/admin/diagnostics/notify-tickets` (`mode: 'preview' | 'send'`, `confirmed: true`).
- **Verification**: `tests/diagnostics-and-reliability.integration.cjs` and `tests/reliability-accessibility-browser.e2e.cjs`.
