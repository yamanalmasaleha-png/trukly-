Active Administrator Scope

Admin: Mod Capabilities

  • Review vendor applications, request corrections, and record approval/rejection recommendations.
  • Moderate pending/reported community posts (approve, reject, hide, restore) with immutable content history.
  • Triage and respond to customer/vendor issues with separated internal vs user-visible notes.
  • Apply bounded temporary safety restrictions (up to 72 hours) on listings when urgent safety/accuracy issues arise.
  • Initiate account invitations at all levels (Mod/Top invitations enter Pending Admin: Top Approval).

Admin: Top Exclusive Controls

  • Final vendor ownership approval, rejection, permanent restriction, suspension, and reactivation.
  • Independent field verification & photo-rights confirmation management.
  • Approve or reject privileged Admin: Mod and Admin: Top invitations (with self-approval blocked).
  • Grant, demote, or revoke administrative roles (with last-Top database & API protection) and suspend accounts.
  • Explicit, reason-audited Act on Behalf of Vendor operational support mode.
