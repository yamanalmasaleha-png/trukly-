FOR FOOD TRUCKS, STANDS & POP-UPS

Your customers should find your food—not give you more admin work.

BiteShark is starting in Sonoma County with practical tools you can update in a minute before or during service.

  • Publish your live spot & service schedule — Share where you’re parked today (with automatic shift expiry) and post upcoming open hours on the Sonoma County directory.
  • Review catering & event inquiries — Receive structured event requests with date, guest count, and neighborhood in one queue.
  • Share browser pickup links — Give waiting customers a private web link (Cooking → Ready) they can check in their open browser tab (no app download; background push alerts are not enabled).
  • Invite counter or kitchen staff — Approved owners can grant scoped staff access via verified email invitation and reach BiteShark support privately.
Account creation and business approval are separate steps: You’ll sign in or create a verified 14+ BiteShark account first, then submit an ownership claim or new listing application for administrator review.
