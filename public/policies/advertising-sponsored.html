5. Advertising, Sponsored Content & Future Revenue Framework (Proposed Draft)

5.1 Current State Today: Zero Ads, Tracking, or Paid Features Active

In the current BiteShark staging build, all 59 Sonoma County listings appear organically based on distance, town, open/schedule status, category, or cuisine filters. BiteShark currently runs no third-party advertising networks, no tracking pixels, no vendor subscriptions, no checkout/billing, and no promotional email or push campaigns.

5.2 Proposed Non-Exploitative Revenue Categories (Future Options)

To sustain local directory operations over time without exploiting user trust or personal data, [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME], doing business as BiteShark reserves room — subject to prior legal review, updated disclosures, and user controls — to offer:

  • Clearly labeled paid placements: Highlighted or featured food-spot placements unmistakably marked with plain-language labels such as “Sponsored” or “Paid Placement.”
  • Optional vendor software subscriptions: Paid operational tools or enhanced vendor profile features under transparent subscription terms.
  • Sponsored local food guides: Event, market, or culinary partner guides that prominently disclose any sponsorship or commercial partnership.
  • Disclosed referral or event-booking commissions: Transparent referral or booking service fees disclosed before a vendor or customer commits to a transaction.
  • Optional promotional communications: Strictly opt-in local food roundups or vendor specials that users can decline or unsubscribe from at any time.

5.3 Proposed Trust, Privacy & Editorial Boundaries

Any future commercial feature on BiteShark is proposed to operate within these strict boundaries:

  • No paid verification or review manipulation: Payment cannot purchase “Verified” or “Approved Owner” status, boost star ratings, generate favorable reviews, or suppress legitimate customer criticism.
  • Operational notifications separate from marketing: Requesting a single-use sign-in code, submitting a booking inquiry, or subscribing to an order-ready pickup pager will never enroll a user in promotional marketing without separate, explicit opt-in consent.
  • No sale of identifiable customer activity or precise location: BiteShark proposes never to sell identifiable customer browsing/booking history or precise geolocation coordinates to data brokers or third-party ad networks.
  • No sensitive-trait targeting: No advertising or recommendations targeted using sensitive personal characteristics (such as health, religion, race/ethnicity, financial distress, or precise location history).
  • No behavioral advertising directed at minors: Zero behavioral or cross-context targeted advertising directed at users known to be under 18.
  • No blanket authorization for unknown future uses: BiteShark does not claim open-ended permission to repurpose personal data for undisclosed commercial schemes.
  • Pre-activation review gate: Any new material data use, monetization model, or third-party SDK requires an internal privacy/security assessment, legal review in the Admin Readiness checklist, updated policy notice, and appropriate user controls before activation.
