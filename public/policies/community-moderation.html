4. Community and Moderation Policy (Preliminary Working Draft)

4.1 Honest Local Food Experiences

BiteShark community posts exist to share genuine firsthand food experiences at Sonoma County trucks, carts, pop-ups, and neighborhood kitchens. Honest criticism about wait times, portions, pricing, or food quality is welcome when respectful and factual; personal attacks, fabricated reviews, and conflicts of interest are not.

4.2 Prohibited Content & Conduct

  • Harassment, hate speech, or threats: Abuse directed at vendor workers, customers, or community members.
  • Doxxing & private information: Publishing personal phone numbers, home addresses, private license plates, payment details, or information about minors.
  • Fake, incentivized, or self-dealing reviews: Owners, staff, competitors, or paid promoters posting undisclosed promotional reviews or coordinated rating manipulation.
  • Spam, unlawful material, or IP infringement: Commercial spam, malware links, or text/media that infringes third-party copyright or trademark rights.

4.3 How Moderation & Reporting Work Today

  • Pre-publication review: Newly submitted community reviews enter a pending moderation queue before appearing publicly on a vendor’s listing.
  • User reporting: Signed-in users can report published posts (inaccurate, spam, harassment/privacy, off-topic, or other), placing the post into the moderator triage queue.
  • Moderators & immutable text history: Authorized Admin: Mod and Admin: Top reviewers may approve, reject, hide, or restore posts with a recorded moderation reason. Moderators do not rewrite a user’s words — original submitted content is preserved internally in an append-only history for accountability and dispute review.
  • Editorial independence: Under BiteShark’s proposed Advertising and Sponsored Content Policy, vendors cannot pay to remove legitimate customer criticism or alter ratings.

4.4 Questions, Corrections & Appeals

Signed-in customers and vendors can submit listing-accuracy questions, moderation inquiries, or account concerns through the in-app Support & issues tool, which separates internal moderator notes from user-visible responses.
