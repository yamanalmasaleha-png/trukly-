7. Cookie and Browser Storage Notice (Preliminary Working Draft)

7.1 Zero Advertising or Analytics Trackers

BiteShark staging sets zero advertising cookies, zero cross-site tracking pixels, and zero analytics or session-recording scripts. We use only strictly necessary HTTP cookies for authentication/security and functional browser storage for user-requested app features.

7.2 HTTP Cookies Used by BiteShark

Cookie Type Attributes & Purpose Lifetime
Strictly Necessary Authentication Session Cookie (__Host- prefix) HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax; Path=/ (host-only; retains an internal legacy session key name for same-origin session continuity). Stores an opaque random session token matched via SHA-256 against PostgreSQL when you sign in. Never set for unauthenticated public discovery requests. 14-day sliding window (30-day maximum lifetime), or cleared immediately upon Sign Out.
Strictly Necessary Age-Gate Cooldown Cookie (__Host-bs_age_gate) HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax; Path=/. Stores only the constant literal value 1 (never a birth date, email, or user identifier) after an under-14 registration attempt is declined, preventing immediate birth-date fishing in the same browser. Server-side network throttling separately uses a minimized, pseudonymous truncated SHA-256 IP rate-limit bucket purged after 24 hours (described in our Draft Privacy Notice). 15 minutes (Max-Age=900).

7.3 Functional Browser Storage & Service Worker

  • localStorage & IndexedDB (device-local functional preferences, caches & tasting notebook): Stores your saved favorite spot IDs for offline/guest viewing, a cached copy of the public Sonoma County food-spot catalog for resilient loading, optional nearby-alert radius preferences, passkey-offer dismissal state, active pickup pager token references on your device, self-reported Food Passport stamps, and any personal notes/photos you save in the local Dish Notebook (IndexedDB). Date of birth, verification codes, and onboarding evidence files are never written to localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB.
  • sessionStorage (tab-scoped preview, invitation handoff & support draft preservation): Used when an authenticated Admin: Mod or Admin: Top enters read-only Customer Preview, Vendor Preview, or audited Act-on-Behalf mode; to temporarily hold a pending single-use invitation reference during sign-in on the same tab; or to preserve an unsent Support & Feedback draft (bs_support_draft_v1) across sign-in or transient network errors; cleared when submitted, manually cleared, exited, or when the browser tab closes.
  • Service Worker (/sw.js): Caches core static shell assets (icons, stylesheets, and app scripts) for progressive web app reliability and handles incoming order-ready web-push notifications if push delivery is enabled in the future (push delivery is disabled on staging).

7.4 Managing Cookies & Local Data

Signing out clears your authentication session cookie and revokes the server session immediately. You can clear localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, and service-worker caches at any time through your browser’s site-data settings (note that clearing site data removes device-only Dish Notebook entries and guest favorites).
