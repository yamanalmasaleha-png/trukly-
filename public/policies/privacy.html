2.2 Categories of Information Processed Today

Feature / Surface Data Processed in Current Application Current Retention / Storage Behavior
Account Registration & Sign-In Normalized email address, opaque account ID, single-use OTP HMAC challenges, SHA-256 hashed session token, truncated IP/user-agent hashes for rate-limiting and session security, and derived 14+ age-eligibility metadata. OTP challenges/rate limits cleaned up after 24 hours; revoked/expired sessions purged after 7 days; account rows persist until disabled/deleted (automated self-service deletion not yet built).
Date of Birth (14+ Check) Self-reported Month, Day, and Year submitted over HTTPS during account creation or one-time existing-account check. No identity documents are collected. Raw date of birth is never stored. Only age_eligibility_status (eligible_14_plus or not_assessed), policy version, assessment timestamp, and self-reported basis are saved. Rejected under-14 attempts create zero accounts, zero OTP challenges, and zero marketing records.
Abuse Cooldown on Rejected Signup HttpOnly cookie __Host-bs_age_gate=1 (15-minute max-age) and truncated SHA-256 IP rate-limit bucket (age_ineligible_ip) to deter automated birth-date fishing. Cookie expires in 15 minutes; IP rate-limit bucket is purged within 24 hours. Stores zero DOB and zero email address.
Location & Nearby Discovery Optional browser geolocation coordinates sent to /api/trucks/nearby when you tap “Near me” to compute distance to Sonoma County listings. Processed transiently in memory to calculate distances; customer geolocation coordinates are not written to user profiles or sold.
Favorites & Dish Notebook Saved truck IDs (stored in PostgreSQL when signed in and cached in browser localStorage); personal dish notebook notes/photos. Dish notebook notes and photos stay locally in your browser’s localStorage and are not uploaded to BiteShark servers.
Event / Catering Booking Inquiries Requester user ID/email, target vendor ID, requested event date, guest count, package interest, and optional note shared with the selected vendor. Stored in bookings for requester and vendor tracking; formal archival/deletion schedule is pending legal review.
Digital Pickup Pagers & Web Push Order ticket label/number, opaque status token, optional web-push endpoint and P-256DH/auth keys when subscribed. Pager tickets expire automatically; web-push delivery is currently disabled on staging (PUSH_PROVIDER_MODE=disabled).
Community Reviews, Reports & Support Issues Display name/nickname, star rating, comment text, moderation history, abuse reports, and private support ticket threads. Moderation preserves original submitted text internally for audit/appeals; retention period pending counsel review.
Vendor Onboarding Applications & Proof Business contact details, permit/license summary, ownership statement, and optional PNG/JPEG/WebP/PDF ownership proof (up to 512 KiB per file, max 3 files). Uploaded onboarding evidence records carry a 90-day retention_expires_at timestamp and scrub raw file bytes upon deletion.

2.3 Proposed Age Policy (14+) & Youth Privacy

Under the operator’s proposed account policy (awaiting legal approval), users aged 13 and younger cannot create accounts; minimum account age is 14. BiteShark does not knowingly create accounts for children aged 13 or younger, does not run behavioral advertising, and does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Important limitation: Self-reported date of birth is not verified age, and restricting account signup to 14+ does not by itself resolve all legal questions regarding teen users (ages 14–17), EU/UK digital-consent rules, or unauthenticated public browsing and anonymous pickup pager use. Those items remain explicitly open for legal review.

2.4 Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

  • Hosting & infrastructure: Application and PostgreSQL database run on a dedicated Linux virtual private server in the United States behind TLS encryption.
  • Map tiles: Interactive discovery maps load street map tiles from OpenStreetMap / CARTO tile endpoints in the browser, which receive standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and browser user-agent) when map view is displayed.
  • Transactional email & push providers: External email delivery is not yet connected on staging (restricted operator test mailbox is active); web push delivery is disabled on staging.
  • No analytics or ad networks: Zero analytics SDKs, ad pixels, session-replay scripts, or data brokers are integrated.

2.5 Current Limitations on Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

Signed-in users can currently open a private support ticket in the app (Profile → Support & issues), and administrators can disable accounts and revoke active sessions. However, automated self-service account deletion, data export, and formal privacy-request (DSAR) workflows are not yet implemented or verified and must be completed before public launch.
