1. Terms of Service (Preliminary Working Draft)

1.1 Operator & Service Description

BiteShark is operated by [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME], doing business as BiteShark (“BiteShark,” “we,” or “us”). BiteShark provides an online food-truck and local food-spot discovery directory currently focused on Sonoma County, California, along with optional account favorites, community reviews, non-payment event/catering booking inquiries, and digital order-pickup pagers operated by participating vendors.

No food preparation, delivery, or payment processing: BiteShark is not a food vendor, caterer, restaurant, food-delivery courier, or payment processor. BiteShark does not prepare food, inspect kitchens, collect payments, issue refunds, or guarantee vendor availability, menu pricing, allergen safety, or health-permit status.

1.2 Proposed Minimum Account Age (14+) & Contractual Capacity

Proposed Product Policy (Awaiting Legal Sign-Off): Users aged 13 and younger cannot create accounts on BiteShark. The minimum age to register a BiteShark customer account is 14.
  • Customer accounts (proposed 14+): Registration requires self-reported date of birth evaluated against the America/Los_Angeles calendar. Self-reported date of birth is an eligibility screen and is not presented as verified age.
  • No implied contractual capacity: Eligibility to create a 14+ customer account does not establish legal capacity or authority to enter binding contracts, submit binding event/catering commitments, purchase future paid services, claim or manage a vendor business, or hold an administrative role.
  • Vendor and administrative accounts: Claiming a listing, accepting the Vendor Terms, or serving as Vendor Staff, Vendor Owner, or Administrator is intended to require an adult of legal age of majority (18+ in California) with actual authority where representing a business (pending final counsel wording).

1.3 Unclaimed Curated Listings vs. Claimed & Verified Spots

Many listings in the BiteShark catalog are curated from publicly observable information and remain unclaimed until a business owner submits an application and receives Admin: Top approval. Hours marked “Est. Open,” locations, cuisines, and menu notes may change without notice. Even where a listing displays an “Approved Owner” or “Verified” indicator, that status reflects BiteShark’s internal catalog check only — never a government health inspection, safety certification, or endorsement.

1.4 Catering / Event Booking Inquiries & Pickup Pagers

  • Booking inquiries are non-binding messages: Submitting an event or catering request through BiteShark transmits an inquiry (date, city/venue, estimated guests, package interest, and note) to a participating vendor. BiteShark does not collect deposits, charge fees, or form a catering contract between you and the vendor; any final event agreement, pricing, insurance, or cancellation policy is arranged directly between requester and vendor.
  • Digital pickup pagers: Participating vendors may issue short-lived pickup codes/links so waiting guests can check when an order is ready or opt into browser web-push alerts (currently disabled on staging). BiteShark does not fulfill food orders or handle order refunds.

1.5 Accounts, Acceptable Use & Safety Restrictions

Sign-in uses single-use 6-digit email verification codes. You are responsible for safeguarding access to your email inbox and active sessions. You may not impersonate a vendor or another person, submit false ownership claims, scrape or overload the service, probe security controls, or post unlawful, abusive, or deceptive content. BiteShark administrators may restrict features, hide content, or suspend accounts to protect users, vendors, or platform integrity.
