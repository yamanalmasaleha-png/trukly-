3. Vendor Terms / Vendor Agreement (Preliminary Working Draft)

3.1 Authority & Contractual Eligibility (Separate from 14+ Customer Signup)

While BiteShark’s proposed customer account rule allows users aged 14 and older to create basic customer accounts, eligibility to create a customer account does not grant contractual capacity or authority to claim, manage, or represent a food business.

Under this proposed Vendor Agreement, any individual submitting a vendor onboarding application, claiming a curated listing, inviting Vendor Staff, or operating the Vendor Operations Hub must be of legal age of majority (18+ in California) with actual authority from the food business to publish operating information and manage customer inquiries.

3.2 Unclaimed Listings, Approved Ownership & Verification

  • Curated unclaimed listings: Unclaimed catalog entries remain informational until an applicant submits an ownership claim or new-listing application and receives explicit Admin: Top approval. Moderators (Admin: Mod) may review applications and request clarifications, but cannot grant final ownership.
  • Separate meanings of status flags:
    • Approved Owner (approved_owner): Admin: Top approved an ownership claim for the listing’s operational workspace.
    • Independently Verified (verified): BiteShark recorded an internal catalog check of public operating presence — not a health-department inspection, permit guarantee, or safety certification.
    • Photo Rights Confirmed (photo_rights_verified): Listing photography is suppressed publicly until photo usage rights are confirmed.

3.3 Vendor Responsibilities & Accuracy

Claimed vendors are solely responsible for maintaining valid local health, mobile-food-facility, commissary, fire, business, and tax permits; publishing truthful hours, live locations, cuisine descriptions, and allergen disclosures; and ensuring they hold full copyright or license rights to any text, logos, or images they submit.

3.4 Team Memberships, Booking Inquiries & Digital Pagers

  • Vendor Staff invitations: Approved Vendor Owners may issue single-use email invitations granting Vendor Staff operational access (schedule, live open/close, booking responses, and pickup pagers). Staff cannot transfer ownership or invite other owners.
  • Event inquiries & pagers: BiteShark transmits non-payment catering/event inquiries and hosts digital order-pickup status pagers. BiteShark is not a party to any catering contract or food sale, collects zero commissions or payments in the current build, and provides no refund or payment processing.
  • Audited Admin: Top support mode: Only Admin: Top may act on behalf of a vendor for explicit support troubleshooting, and every such action requires a recorded support reason and audit-log entry.

3.5 Granular Safety & Accuracy Restrictions

To protect customers without unnecessarily stranding open catering inquiries or active pickup pagers, BiteShark may apply scoped restrictions — including pausing new bookings, pausing new pickup pagers, requesting listing corrections, hiding a listing from public discovery, or suspending vendor workspace access. Admin: Mod may apply bounded temporary safety holds (up to 72 hours); permanent restrictions and reactivations require Admin: Top.
